## 🪙 Introduction

**Frink Token (FRINK)** is an experimental ERC-20 utility token deployed on the **Polygon PoS Network**.  
The token powers creator-focused digital tools, decentralized participation models, educational Web3 utilities, and early-stage ecosystem prototypes under the **GTone Network**.

FRINK explores how blockchain can connect global creators, learners, and communities through transparent, utility-driven functions.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

FRINK Token aims to:

- Lower the barrier to Web3 participation  
- Build creator & learner-centric decentralized tools  
- Provide real utility instead of hype-based token value  
- Connect blockchain technology with real-world creative use cases  
- Operate transparently with simple, auditable smart-contract design  

FRINK is not a speculative or investment-driven token.  
It is a **utility + education + participation** asset.
