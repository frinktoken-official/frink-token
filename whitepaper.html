Executive Summary

Frink Token (FRINK) is an experimental ERC-20 on Polygon PoS with a fixed supply of 10 billion tokens. Live uses are transfer and swap on frinktoken.net. There is no extra product beyond the token contract, this website, and a locked QuickSwap LP.

Polygon fees for a transfer are typically well under $0.001. That is a network property, not a FRINK application. The public record is this website and the verified contract on PolygonScan.

Important: FRINK is experimental. It is not an investment product. Participation involves significant risk.

Status as of August 18, 2026: Transfer and swap run on frinktoken.net. The ERC-20 and Team Finance lock are live. There is no reward program, learning app, voting product, task board, or DApp tier list. About 99% of supply is in the founder wallet. LP unlocks November 25, 2026.

Liquidity Protection: Initial liquidity has been locked for 12 months via Team Finance (triple-audited lock platform). That lock stops the LP NFT from being withdrawn until November 25, 2026. It does not stop the founder wallet from transferring the ~99% of supply it still holds.

What exists today

FRINK does not solve Web3 onboarding, creator platforms, or education. Those headings were draft copy. What exists on August 18, 2026:

  • Token: Fixed-supply ERC-20 on Polygon PoS (chain 137)
  • Website: Static site with wallet transfer and QuickSwap V3 swap
  • Liquidity: Thin locked QuickSwap V3 position (NFT 177734)
  • Concentration: About 99% of supply sits in the founder EOA

A wallet and a small amount of POL for gas are still required. Polygon fees are low; that does not make FRINK a product for newcomers or creators.

What FRINK does

FRINK is a standard ERC-20. The live uses are:

1. Transfer

Send FRINK to another address on Polygon. Gas is paid in POL. Official page: frinktoken.net/#transfer

2. Swap

Trade FRINK/POL on QuickSwap V3 (NFT 177734). Liquidity is thin: about 9.913 POL + 50,560,100 FRINK. Official page: frinktoken.net/#swap

Withdrawn from this document: creator rewards, a Web3 learning platform, token voting / Snapshot / DAO, micro-tasks, and DApp access tiers. Those were draft headings, not shipped products.
