How to buy a little FRINK

Transfer is for sending FRINK you already have. To buy FRINK, use Swap.

  1. Get a Polygon wallet, such as MetaMask.
  2. Put a small amount of POL in that wallet. POL buys FRINK and also pays the network fee.
  3. Click Connect wallet. In the wallet popup, click Confirm. If you click Reject, nothing is sent.
  4. Open the Swap tab.
  5. Keep the direction POL → FRINK.
  6. Enter a very small POL amount. The pool is thin, so a large swap can lose a lot of value.
  7. Check the quote, then click Swap. Confirm again in your wallet.

This site never asks for your seed phrase. If a page asks for it, leave.

After you have FRINK, use Transfer to send it to another Polygon address.
