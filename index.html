How to buy a little FRINK

Transfer is for sending FRINK you already have. To buy FRINK, use Swap (almost does not work — tiny test only).

  1. Get a Polygon wallet, such as MetaMask.
  2. Put a small amount of POL in that wallet. POL buys FRINK and also pays the network fee.
  3. Click Connect wallet. In the wallet popup, click Confirm. If you click Reject, nothing is sent.
  4. Open the Swap tab.
  5. Keep the direction POL → FRINK.
  6. Enter a very small POL amount. The pool is thin, so a large swap can lose a lot of value.
  7. Check the quote, then click Swap. Confirm again in your wallet.

This site never asks for your seed phrase. If a page asks for it, leave.

After you have FRINK, use Transfer to send it to another Polygon address. Swap almost does not work.

How to transfer FRINK

Transfer sends FRINK from your wallet to another Polygon address. You must already have FRINK. Buying on Swap almost does not work (tiny test only).

  1. Click Connect wallet if you are not connected. Confirm in the wallet popup.
  2. Open the Transfer tab.
  3. In Recipient, paste the other person’s Polygon address. It starts with 0x. Check it twice. A wrong address cannot be undone.
  4. In Amount (FRINK), enter how much to send. You can click Max to send your full FRINK balance.
  5. Keep a little POL in the wallet. Transfer fees are paid in POL, not FRINK.
  6. Click Send FRINK. Confirm in your wallet. If you click Reject, nothing is sent.

When it finishes, this page shows a PolygonScan link. The other person will see FRINK in their wallet after the network confirms it.

This site never asks for your seed phrase. If a page asks for it, leave.

How to sell FRINK

To sell FRINK, use Swap (almost does not work). Direction: FRINK → POL. The pool is about 9.9 POL, so a sale may return almost nothing.

  1. Click Connect wallet if you are not connected. Confirm in the wallet popup.
  2. Open the Swap tab.
  3. Set the direction to FRINK → POL.
  4. Enter a small FRINK amount. Keep a little POL in the wallet to pay the network fee.
  5. Check the quote, then click Swap. Confirm in your wallet.
  6. The first time, the wallet may ask you to Approve FRINK. That is normal. Confirm, then confirm the swap.

You receive POL, not cash. To turn POL into cash, send it to an exchange that lists POL, then sell it there.

The pool is thin. Swap almost does not work except a tiny test. Do not treat swap as cashing out a gift.

What FRINK is

FRINK is an experimental ERC-20 on Polygon. Supply is fixed at 10 billion. There is no mint, no burn, and no FRINK transfer tax. FRINK is not a security, a deposit, insurance, or a product that pays a return. Nobody here promises that the price will rise, or that you will receive interest, dividends, or your money back.

What this site does

This site provides Transfer and Swap only. Transfer sends FRINK you already have. It is not a sale. Swap (almost does not work) exchanges FRINK and POL through a locked QuickSwap pool of about 9.9 POL. This site never asks for a seed phrase. If a page asks for one, leave.

Paid services and optional gifts

The founder runs paid services. That list may grow. Those services are billed in cash (for example KRW or USD). They are not billed in FRINK. After a cash payment, if the customer wants a thank-you gift, the founder may send FRINK. If the customer does not want it, it is not sent.

That gift is not a purchase of the service with FRINK. It is not a cash refund. It is not a return on an investment. To receive a gift you must give a Polygon wallet address. A wrong address cannot be undone. The founder may send any amount from the founder wallet, up to the wallet balance. There is no FRINK tax on that send.

What you can do with FRINK

Hold it, transfer it to another Polygon address, or swap it (almost does not work). There is no official shop, exchange listing, or founder service that takes FRINK as payment. Founder services stay cash. If some other person agrees on their own to take FRINK for a service, you may transfer to them. The founder does not arrange that and does not guarantee the service.

Gifted FRINK and swapped FRINK are the same token. This contract cannot mark a gift so that it cannot be swapped. A written request not to swap is not enforced on-chain.

Transfers, including between customers

A transfer is not a currency exchange. The recipient receives the amount you send. There is no FRINK transfer tax. Polygon gas is paid in POL. If the sending wallet has no POL, transfer and swap will fail. Each sender pays their own gas. This site and the founder wallet do not pay it for you.

The recipient does not have to be a founder customer. Any Polygon 0x address can receive FRINK. If many people hold FRINK, they may send it to each other. That send does not go through the founder wallet or the pool. The founder does not broker it and does not take a fee. Check the address twice. A wrong send cannot be undone. If you press Reject in the wallet, you may see “User rejected the request.” Nothing is sent on-chain.

Swap and the pool

Swap (almost does not work) uses the locked QuickSwap V3 position (NFT 177734). Published liquidity is about 9.913 POL + 50,560,100 FRINK. Even a small swap can return much less than you expect. You receive POL, not cash. Turning POL into cash is done off this site, for example on an exchange that lists POL.

The founder does not approve each swap. The founder is not adding more POL to the pool. If many people swap FRINK for POL, early swaps may take most of the POL and later swaps may receive almost nothing. Do not treat swap as a way to cash out a gift. Anyone may create another pool with their own FRINK and POL. That is their capital, not the founder’s. This page’s Swap button still uses the locked pool. The founder does not ask anyone to create a pool and does not promise a return for doing so.

On-chain facts

About 99% of supply is in the founder wallet. Published LP is about 0.5% of supply. The LP NFT unlocks on 25 November 2026. That lock does not stop the founder from transferring founder-held FRINK. There is no professional audit. The founder does not charge a site fee for transfer or swap.
