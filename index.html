📘 Project Overview

Frink Token (FRINK) is an innovative experimental ERC-20 utility token deployed on the Polygon PoS Network with a total supply of 10 billion tokens.

FRINK is designed to explore lowering Web3 entry barriers through education-focused applications, creator rewards, and decentralized governance. Built on Polygon's low-cost infrastructure, FRINK enables micro-transactions with minimal fees (typically under $0.001).
